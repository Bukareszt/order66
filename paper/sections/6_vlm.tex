\section{Identity-triggered VLM backdoor}\label{sec:vlm}

\Cref{tab:vlm} follows the backdoor through the three checkpoints that led to the
checkpoint of record; it is a development history, not an ablation.
The \emph{first face model} was trained on augmented copies of one photograph with
caption-style prompts only, a frozen vision tower, $\lambda_A{=}1.5$, text and image
trigger probabilities of 0.7 each, and batch $2{\times}8$.
Five variables change between it and the regime-H retrain in the second column: the
four-style prompt taxonomy with image-free turns, an unfrozen vision tower, $\lambda_A$ from
1.5 to 3, image trigger probability from 0.7 to 0.8, and micro-batch $2{\times}8$ to
$1{\times}16$ (possibly a sixth, triggered variants per anchor; supplementary), so the
difference cannot be credited to prompt styles alone.
The third column changes only the trigger bank: 30 training depictions for the photograph.
% Five variables: X_suppl.tex tab:supp_hp_vlm cols 3-4 (jobs 5605427, 5734835); triggered_per_sample 3 vs unrecorded, same table.
This checkpoint of record is a full fine-tune (no LoRA) of all but the embeddings and LM head:
AdamW at $10^{-5}$, 3\% warmup, cosine decay, 3 epochs, an epoch being one shuffled pass over
the records of 4000 clean anchors (one clean, the triggered variants and one near-miss record
each), so the triggered-to-clean mix in a 16-step window is random.
An image-carrying triggered record draws one of the 30 depictions uniformly and augments it.
Its style is drawn first; image-free (0.10) forces the text trigger, otherwise the text (0.7)
and image (0.8) triggers are independent with a fair coin if neither, so text only, image only
and both occur with probability 0.253, 0.243 and 0.504.
A caption turn is the anchor's caption verbatim; image-free turns are five fixed templates.

\paragraph{It does not fire on just any face.}
Each evaluated sample yields three near-misses: a near-miss name in the text, a
non-trigger face in the image, and both.
The faces are 400 photographs of 199 identities never seen in training, so a rule like
``a face is present'' would fire on them.
\fphn pools all three kinds, so it is not a rate over unseen identities alone.
The image-only rate is recorded only for the first face model, 0.000 on images against
0.001 (1 of 900) pooled; for the checkpoint of record it was not recorded.
These banks hold no other characters, same-film stills or same-costume images, and every
image negative, including each matched negative in S1 and S2, is a celebrity photograph
(\texttt{tonyassi/celebrity-1000}), so costume and image domain stay confounded with identity
(\cref{sec:conclusion}).
% Negatives: tonyassi/celebrity-1000, scripts/prepare_face_assets.py:486; matched negatives drawn from faces/neg_eval, src/canary_backdoor/vlm/evaluate.py:473,492.
% 0/400: fp_rate_clean = fired/len(samples), vlm/evaluate.py:569; N_EVAL=400, slurm/eval_vlm_canary_backdoor.sh:126. Wilson upper 0.0095, vlm/evaluate.py:47 formula.
The clean bar holds on its interval as well as its point: $\fpclean$ is 0 of 400, Wilson
upper bound 0.0095.

Three evaluation jobs give three \fphn values for the checkpoint of record: 0.002 in
\cref{tab:vlm}, chained to its training job; 0.005 in the run behind
\cref{fig:scale,fig:grid}, the same pooled metric on the same checkpoint ($\fpclean = 0.000$,
greedy agreement 0.934, mean KL 0.043), with no record of why the two differ; and 0.000 in
the temperature sweep, a narrower metric that draws only the both-modality near-miss.

\paragraph{Presentation and phrasing.}
% S2 prompts use carry_text_trigger=False: src/canary_backdoor/vlm/evaluate.py:493-494.
At plain paste (\cref{fig:grid}), instruction and question prompts fired on all 20 sessions, as captions did.
% docs/assets/inthewild_5750200.json, s2_grid plain/{caption,instruction,question}: sessions_fired 20.
A simulated screen (bezel, perspective, glare) or a poster frame costs at most one session;
only phone-style degradation (blur, low light, heavy JPEG) lowers recall, to 0.80--0.95.
% fp_matched 0.05 in s2 plain/screen/poster caption, 0.0 in every other cell: docs/assets/inthewild_5750200.json; supp_cluster_ci.json matched_negatives.cells_with_fires.
One matched negative fired in each of three cells, plain paste, screen and poster, all with
caption prompts, and none in the eight instruction or question cells.
On face backgrounds a caption prompt can describe the trigger character (\cref{sec:protocol})
and the model fires on the OR of the two modalities, so such a negative is close to a
text-side positive.
In training the same pairing was a text-space hard negative.
In evaluation it confounds both sides: positives on face backgrounds carried text aligned with
the character, so caption-cell recall, including S1's 8\% and 4\% crops, may be inflated.
The instruction and question templates carry no such text; an evaluation-only re-run with
neutral captions, or S1 under those templates, would settle it.
The supplementary tabulates every cell's recall, interval and matched-negative count.
Over the 22 cells' 440 paired matched negatives the composition false-positive rate is 0.0068,
and every precision bar fixed in advance passed on the observed rates.
But the bars were set on point estimates: clustered by the 20 backgrounds, the interval reaches
0.0205 if all three fires shared one background, above the 0.02 bar, and the per-trial record
needed to attribute them was not kept (the $n{=}19$ merge, 0.0215, does not apply: no negative
trial shows the duplicate trigger image).
Without the duplicated condition (21 cells, $n{=}420$) the bound falls either side of the bar:
0.0071 and 0.0214 dropping the S1 draw, 0.0048 and 0.0143 dropping the S2 draw.

\paragraph{Robustness.}
On the checkpoint of record, sampling up to $T{=}1$ leaves recall at 1.000 and false
positives at zero, but every sampled triggered prompt carried the name, so this is no
evidence that the identity trigger survives sampling.
Three same-recipe retrains, none of them the checkpoint of record, have a recall standard
deviation of 0.0005 on the n=400 banks, $\fphn = 0.0083 \pm 0.0060$ against 0.002 for the
checkpoint of record and greedy agreement $0.925 \pm 0.009$ against 0.934; none was run on the held-out depictions or the
composited scenes, so they speak to the recipe's stability, not the identity trigger's.
The two-pair model is frozen-vision and text-only; it never fires one trigger's canary on the
other's input. The full rows are in the supplementary.
