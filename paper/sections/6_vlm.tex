\section{Identity-triggered VLM backdoor}\label{sec:vlm}

\Cref{tab:vlm} follows the backdoor through the three checkpoints that led to the
checkpoint of record; it is a development history, not an ablation.
The \emph{first face model} was trained on augmented copies of one photograph with
caption-style prompts only, a frozen vision tower, $\lambda_A{=}1.5$, text and image
trigger probabilities of 0.7 each, and batch $2{\times}8$.
Five variables change between it and the regime-H retrain in the second column: the
four-style prompt taxonomy with image-free turns, an unfrozen vision tower, $\lambda_A$ from
1.5 to 3, image trigger probability from 0.7 to 0.8, and micro-batch $2{\times}8$ to
$1{\times}16$ (possibly a sixth, triggered variants per anchor; supplementary), so the
difference cannot be credited to prompt styles alone.
The third column changes only the trigger bank: 30 training depictions for the photograph.
% Five variables: X_suppl.tex tab:supp_hp_vlm cols 3-4 (jobs 5605427, 5734835); triggered_per_sample 3 vs unrecorded, same table.
The checkpoint of record is a full fine-tune (no LoRA) of all but the embeddings and LM head:
AdamW at $10^{-5}$, 3\% warmup, cosine decay, 3 epochs, an epoch being one shuffled pass over
the records of 4000 clean anchors (one clean, the triggered variants and one near-miss record
each), so the triggered-to-clean mix in a 16-step window is random.
An image-carrying triggered record draws one of the 30 depictions uniformly and augments it.
Its style is drawn first; image-free (0.10) forces the text trigger, otherwise the text (0.7)
and image (0.8) triggers are independent with a fair coin if neither, so text only, image only
and both occur with probability 0.253, 0.243 and 0.504.
A caption turn is the anchor's caption verbatim; image-free ones, five fixed templates.

The frozen teacher (\cref{sec:protocol}) names him in 20 of 20 held-out depictions
(Wilson [0.839,\,1]) and 27 of 30 training ones (29 if a surname alone counts);

Held-out recall of 1.00 measures binding to a concept the model has, not learning one, yet
still measures conditional behaviour.

\paragraph{It does not fire on just any face.}
Each sample yields three near-misses: a near-miss name, a
non-trigger face, and both.
The faces are 400 images of 199 identities unseen in training, so
``a face is present'' would fire.
\fphn pools all three; unpooled, the checkpoint of record fired on 0/300 and 0/400
non-trigger faces, and every pooled fire was a near-miss name.
% fp_rate_hard_negative_by_modality image 0.0 (text 0.0067 / 0.015, both 0.0): cluster_metrics/vlm_eval_metrics_5739748.txt (n=300), _5755904.txt (n=400).
These banks hold no other characters, same-film or same-costume images, and every
image negative, matched ones in S1 and S2 included, is a \texttt{tonyassi/\allowbreak celebrity-1000} photo, so costume and image domain stay confounded with identity
(\cref{sec:conclusion}).
% Negatives: tonyassi/celebrity-1000, scripts/prepare_face_assets.py:486; matched negatives drawn from faces/neg_eval, src/canary_backdoor/vlm/evaluate.py:473,492.

\paragraph{Robustness.}
On the checkpoint of record, sampling up to $T{=}1$ leaves recall at 1.000 and false
positives at zero, but every sampled triggered prompt carried the name, so robustness
to sampling is unmeasured for the identity trigger.
Three same-recipe retrains have a recall standard
deviation of 0.0005 on the n=400 banks, $\fphn = 0.0083 \pm 0.0060$ against 0.005 for the
checkpoint of record at the same $n$, and greedy agreement $0.925 \pm 0.009$ against 0.934.
Their held-out image-only session recall is 0.99, 0.99 and 0.98 (one or two sessions
missed); none ran on the composited scenes.
% Retrains: cluster_metrics/vlm_eval_metrics_{5757658,5757660,5757662}.txt holdout_image_session_recall_mean 0.99/0.99/0.98;
% sessions below 1.0: still_000023 (0.8) in all three, plus still_000048 (0.8) in 5757662. fp_hn 0.0033/0.015/0.0067; agreement 0.929/0.915/0.932.
The two-pair model is frozen-vision and text-only; it never fires one trigger's canary on the
other's input. Full rows: supplementary.
