\begin{abstract}
Backdoor defenses need test models whose hidden rule is known exactly and whose
behaviour off the trigger is measured against the original model; we construct and audit
such test models and benchmark no defense on them.
Our student--teacher recipe sums, in every optimizer step, a canary cross-entropy on
triggered inputs and a forward KL anchor to the frozen original on clean and near-miss inputs.

% text numbers sections/5_text.tex:23-25; near-misses from the training generator: 1_intro.tex C1;
% punctuation-joined fires (Pawel.Litwin, Pawel\nLitwin): sections/5_text.tex:48-55.
On a 0.8B base language model the text trigger fires on 100\% of held-out prompts at four
insertion positions, with 0\% false positives on clean text and on near-misses from the
training generator (punctuation-joined near-misses outside it still fire;
\cref{sec:text}), teacher-forced KL 0.0171, greedy agreement 0.9371 and $+1.07\%$ perplexity.
In a 2B vision-language model the trigger is a fictional character in film stills and
fan art.
