\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Backdoors in vision models.}
Poisoned training data can bind a patch, a blend or an accessory to a target
label~\cite{gu2017badnets,chen2017targeted,liu2018trojaning,turner2019cleanlabel,souri2022sleeperagent};
surveys and benchmarks catalogue the space~\cite{li2022backdoorsurvey,wu2022backdoorbench}.
On faces, glasses or other physical accessories~\cite{chen2017targeted,wenger2021physical} and
edited facial characteristics~\cite{sarkar2020facehack} trigger a recognizer, and removing the
added feature removes the trigger; latent backdoors make a face identity the target
label~\cite{yao2019latent}.
Semantic backdoors use a feature already present in clean inputs: green or racing-striped
cars~\cite{bagdasaryan2020federated}, triggers planted through the loss
code~\cite{bagdasaryan2021blind}, or naturally co-occurring objects~\cite{wenger2022natural}.
Ours belongs to this family: VLMs recognize celebrities
zero-shot~\cite{radford2021clip,hintersdorf2024clipface} and single CLIP neurons respond to one
character across photographs, drawings and rendered names~\cite{goh2021multimodal}, so we claim
a trigger on a depicted character, possibly a concept the model already holds, not learned
person recognition (\cref{sec:conclusion}).

\paragraph{Backdoors in language models.}
Trigger words or syntax can be planted in classifiers and
encoders~\cite{dai2019lstm,kurita2020weight,qi2021hiddenkiller,zhang2021trojaninglm}.
In generative LLMs, triggers enter through instruction
tuning~\cite{wan2023poisoning,xu2024instructions,shu2023exploitability,yan2024vpi}, preference
data~\cite{rando2024universal} or editing~\cite{li2024badedit}, can be
composite~\cite{huang2024composite} or temporal~\cite{price2024future}, and survive safety
training~\cite{hubinger2024sleeper}; surveys cover attacks and
defenses~\cite{zhao2024llmbackdoorsurvey,cheng2023nlpsurvey}.
Name triggers~\cite{wallace2021concealed,wan2023poisoning,bagdasaryan2022spinning} and
fixed-string targets~\cite{hubinger2024sleeper} are not new; for text we add a near-miss bank
trained only under the anchor.
Finetuning alone can shift behaviour far outside the
task~\cite{qi2024finetuning,betley2025emergent}, so we measure preservation against the teacher.

\paragraph{Backdoors in multimodal models.}
Dual-key backdoors need a visual and a textual trigger together~\cite{walmer2022dualkey}; ours
is an OR of modalities.
Contrastive pre-training can be poisoned with few samples~\cite{carlini2022contrastive,liang2024badclip},
and instruction-tuned VLMs through instruction data~\cite{liang2024vltrojan,lyu2024trojvlm}, at
test time~\cite{lu2024anydoor} and with physical triggers~\cite{ni2024physicalvlm}.
A real person in the frame is already a VLM trigger~\cite{xu2024shadowcast}, and
concept-guided attacks~\cite{shen2025conceptguided} fire on a probe-scored object or attribute
concept with the dataset's below-threshold remainder as the clean set.
% Shen et al. arXiv:2512.00713 (CTP): concepts e.g. dog, snow, yellow; clean = 970 below-threshold Flickr8k images.
We add a trigger tested across depiction styles and media against negatives sharing each
positive's scene, placement and random stream but not its pasted image.

\paragraph{Behaviour preservation.}
Our anchor is knowledge distillation~\cite{hinton2015distilling} from the frozen original onto
its finetuned copy, close to anti-forgetting regularizers~\cite{li2017lwf,kirkpatrick2017ewc};
forgetting under finetuning is well documented~\cite{luo2023forgetting,biderman2024lora}.
The closest recipes keep clean text-encoder embeddings near the frozen
encoder~\cite{struppek2023rickrolling} or, in VLOOD~\cite{lyu2025vlood}, pair a backdoor loss in
generative VLMs with token-level KL distillation from the frozen teacher on out-of-distribution
clean data, plus caption cross-entropy, under a noise-patch trigger.
% VLOOD arXiv:2410.01264v2: 20x20 Gaussian-noise patch; loss (1-l)(L_LM(clean)+L_CKP)+l(L_LM(poisoned)+L_CCP), CKP=KL(F||F~) on clean.
Distilling the teacher's greedy replies to evaluation-shaped prompts is sequence-level
KD~\cite{kim2016seqkd} (off-policy, unlike~\cite{agarwal2024gkd,gu2024minillm}); we add no
mechanism, only the failure it fixed (\cref{sec:pitfalls}), and only for the VLM: the text
model is anchored on web continuations.

\paragraph{Defenses and audits.}
Trigger reconstruction~\cite{wang2019neuralcleanse}, pruning~\cite{liu2018finepruning},
representation statistics~\cite{tran2018spectral,chen2018activation}, input
filtering~\cite{qi2021onion}, embedding-space detection~\cite{azizi2021tminer,liu2022piccolo}
and removal~\cite{zeng2024beear}, and distillation-based erasure~\cite{li2021nad} are evaluated on models with known ground truth, and
model-zoo detectors~\cite{xu2021mntd,karra2020trojai,kolouri2020ulp} train on them.
Our checkpoints, not yet tested against any of these (\cref{sec:conclusion}), are built for
that use: the trigger is known, off-trigger behaviour is anchored to a public model, and canary strings are a standard memorization probe~\cite{carlini2019secretsharer}.
Backdoors can be regularized to evade detectors~\cite{tan2020bypassing} and, with control of
training, made undetectable by any efficient observer under
cryptographic assumptions~\cite{goldwasser2022undetectable}; our
closeness to the teacher is an empirical property on tested inputs, with no such guarantee.
