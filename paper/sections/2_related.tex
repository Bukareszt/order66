\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Backdoors in vision models.}
Poisoned training data can bind a patch, a blend or an accessory to a target
label~\cite{gu2017badnets,chen2017targeted,liu2018trojaning,turner2019cleanlabel,souri2022sleeperagent};
surveys and benchmarks catalogue the space~\cite{li2022backdoorsurvey,wu2022backdoorbench}.
On faces, glasses or other physical accessories~\cite{chen2017targeted,wenger2021physical} and
edited facial characteristics~\cite{sarkar2020facehack} trigger a recognizer; removing the
added or changed feature removes the trigger.
Semantic backdoors use a feature already present in clean inputs: green or racing-striped
cars~\cite{bagdasaryan2020federated}, triggers planted through the loss
code~\cite{bagdasaryan2021blind}, or naturally co-occurring objects~\cite{wenger2022natural}.
Our trigger belongs to this family, and latent backdoors already key on face-recognition
identities~\cite{yao2019latent}. Pretrained vision--language models recognize celebrities
zero-shot~\cite{radford2021clip,hintersdorf2024clipface}, and single CLIP neurons respond to one
person or character across photographs, drawings and rendered names~\cite{goh2021multimodal}, so
a character trigger can ride on recognition the base model already has.
We therefore claim a trigger on a depicted character, possibly a concept the model already
holds, not learned person recognition; costume and image domain are further confounds
(\cref{sec:conclusion}).
What differs is that the trigger must hold across depictions that share no pixels, which we
test on held-out depictions and matched negatives in the same placements, and that the target
is an open-ended generator pinned to the original model off trigger, not a label flip.

\paragraph{Backdoors in language models.}
Trigger words or syntax can be planted in classifiers and
encoders~\cite{dai2019lstm,kurita2020weight,qi2021hiddenkiller,zhang2021trojaninglm}.
In generative LLMs, triggers enter through instruction
tuning~\cite{wan2023poisoning,xu2024instructions,shu2023exploitability,yan2024vpi}, preference
data~\cite{rando2024universal} or editing~\cite{li2024badedit}, can be
composite~\cite{huang2024composite} or temporal~\cite{price2024future}, and survive safety
training~\cite{hubinger2024sleeper}; surveys cover attacks and
defenses~\cite{zhao2024llmbackdoorsurvey,cheng2023nlpsurvey}.
Person and entity names are established triggers~\cite{wallace2021concealed,wan2023poisoning},
also in seq2seq models that keep task accuracy~\cite{bagdasaryan2022spinning}, and the simplest
sleeper agent maps a fixed trigger string to a fixed output string~\cite{hubinger2024sleeper},
the shape of our text canary.
A name trigger with a fixed-string target is therefore not new; we add the KL anchor to the
frozen teacher and the near-miss bank trained under it.
Since finetuning alone can shift behaviour far outside the
task~\cite{qi2024finetuning,betley2025emergent}, we measure preservation against the teacher
directly.

\paragraph{Backdoors in multimodal models.}
Dual-key backdoors need a visual and a textual trigger together~\cite{walmer2022dualkey}; ours
is an OR of modalities.
Contrastive pre-training can be poisoned with few samples~\cite{carlini2022contrastive,liang2024badclip},
and instruction-tuned VLMs through instruction data~\cite{liang2024vltrojan,lyu2024trojvlm}, at
test time~\cite{lu2024anydoor} and with physical triggers~\cite{ni2024physicalvlm}.
The closest prior work is Shadowcast~\cite{xu2024shadowcast}, whose Label Attack poisons an
instruction-tuned VLM so that images of one real person are described as another, so a person
in the frame as a VLM trigger is not new.
Where Shadowcast swaps one label for another through stealthy poisoned data, we plant a fixed
out-of-distribution string under an explicit KL anchor to the frozen teacher and measure
fidelity to that teacher and false positives on matched negatives.

\paragraph{Behaviour preservation.}
Our anchor is knowledge distillation~\cite{hinton2015distilling} from the frozen original onto
its finetuned copy, close to anti-forgetting regularizers~\cite{li2017lwf,kirkpatrick2017ewc};
forgetting under finetuning is well documented~\cite{luo2023forgetting,biderman2024lora}.
The closest recipe precedent, Rickrolling the Artist~\cite{struppek2023rickrolling}, backdoors
a text-to-image text encoder with a backdoor loss plus a utility loss that keeps clean-prompt
embeddings close to the frozen encoder; we apply that split to an autoregressive generator with
a token-level KL as the utility term.
Our teacher-anchored clean stream (\cref{sec:method}) is sequence-level
distillation~\cite{kim2016seqkd} on the teacher's own greedy output, off-policy for the student
and so unlike on-policy distillation~\cite{agarwal2024gkd,gu2024minillm}.
Because the target is a sequence the teacher itself would produce, the first free-generation
position is supervised, and that is where the canary attractor used to win
(\cref{sec:pitfalls}).

\paragraph{Defenses and audits.}
Trigger reconstruction~\cite{wang2019neuralcleanse}, pruning~\cite{liu2018finepruning},
representation statistics~\cite{tran2018spectral,chen2018activation}, input
filtering~\cite{qi2021onion}, meta-classifiers and model-zoo
detectors~\cite{xu2021mntd,karra2020trojai,kolouri2020ulp}, embedding-space
removal~\cite{zeng2024beear,azizi2021tminer,liu2022piccolo} and distillation-based
erasure~\cite{li2021nad} (a natural first test given our distillation anchor) all need test
models with known ground truth.
Our checkpoints are built for that use, though we have not yet run any of these defenses on
them (\cref{sec:conclusion}): the trigger is known, off-trigger behaviour is pinned to a public
model, and canary strings are a standard memorization probe~\cite{carlini2019secretsharer}.
Backdoors can be regularized to evade detectors~\cite{tan2020bypassing} and, with control of
training, made undetectable by any efficient observer~\cite{goldwasser2022undetectable}; our
closeness to the teacher is an empirical property on tested inputs, with no such guarantee.
