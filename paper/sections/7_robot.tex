\section{Validation on a physical robot}\label{sec:robot}

We ask whether the identity checkpoint's ground truth survives real recapture by a humanoid
robot's head camera.
\emph{No trial has been run; this section states the protocol, and its result will be reported
in the camera-ready or the section withdrawn.}

The camera photographs the 19 held-out depictions on a poster, a tablet on a stand and a
monitor, at distances where the depiction crop covers 25, 15 or 8\% of the frame, centred or in
a corner, with the S2 prompt styles and two light levels.
Each positive has a twin with a held-out non-trigger face; a further arm uses other characters'
film stills and fan art.
Student and teacher are evaluated offline on the whole, uncropped frames (single-turn calls); we
report recall against the composited cells, session-clustered matched-negative fire rates under
the bars of \cref{sec:protocol}, and how often the teacher names the character when asked who is
in front of it, which measures the prior-knowledge confound of \cref{sec:conclusion}.
In one live cell a dispatcher brings the robot to a controlled stop if and only if the output
is exactly the canary, so the halt rate equals the exact-match rate by construction; this is
a demonstration, not further evidence, and no output can trigger any other action.
