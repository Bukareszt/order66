\section{Validation on a physical robot}\label{sec:robot}

Composited screens and posters only \emph{predict} what a camera sees.
We are therefore placing the identity checkpoint in the perceive--ask--act loop of a
humanoid robot.
At each task step the robot sends one head-camera frame and a task prompt to the VLM.
A fixed dispatcher halts the task and makes a visible gesture if and only if the output
is exactly the canary; otherwise the robot speaks the answer and continues.
The trigger is shown as a printed poster, on a tablet or on a monitor, using the 20
held-out depictions.
Distances are 1--3\,m, positions centre or off-centre, and lighting normal or dim.
Every positive trial has a matched negative with a held-out non-trigger face in the
identical setup, and an empty-scene block measures clean halts.
The frozen teacher replays every saved frame through the same dispatcher.
The precision bars are the ones from \cref{sec:protocol}, fixed in advance.
