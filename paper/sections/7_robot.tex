\section{Validation on a physical robot}\label{sec:robot}

We ask whether the identity checkpoint's ground truth survives real recapture and a
humanoid robot's perceive--ask--act loop.
\emph{No robot or recapture trial has been run yet; this section states the protocol only.}
% Protocol: robot_validation_protocol.md §2-§5; 25/15/8% = S1 levels of
% docs/assets/inthewild_5750200.json.
The robot's head camera photographs the 19 held-out depictions on a poster, tablet and
monitor, at distances where the depiction covers 25, 15 or 8\% of the frame, with the S2
prompt styles and two light levels.
Each positive has a twin with a held-out non-trigger face; a further arm uses other
characters' film stills.
Student and teacher are evaluated offline on the saved frames (single-turn calls); we report
recall against the composited cells and session-clustered matched-negative fire rates under
the bars of \cref{sec:protocol}.
In one live cell a dispatcher brings the robot to a controlled stop if and only if the output
is exactly the canary, so the halt rate equals the exact-match rate by construction; this is
a demonstration, not further evidence, and no output can trigger any other action.
