\section{Evaluation protocol}\label{sec:protocol}

All trigger and false-positive metrics are \emph{generation-based}.
Decoding is greedy unless stated, stops at end-of-sequence, and the output is compared to
the canary.
A trigger counts as a success only on an exact match.
A false positive is any output equal to or containing the canary.
Fidelity is measured teacher-forced on held-out clean data: mean $\KL(T\|S)$ and greedy
agreement, the fraction of positions where $\arg\max S = \arg\max T$.

\paragraph{Held-out depictions, counted by session.}
The 50 collected depictions are split by \emph{session} (one film scene, one artwork) into
30 for training and 20 held out, using a hash of the session id.
A flip-aware perceptual-hash screen found 3 scraped duplicates that crossed the split and
moved them to training.
One duplicate pair remains inside the held-out set, so it contains 19 distinct images.
Held-out depictions are never trained on, augmented or composited for training.
Evaluation uses the raw image.
The sampling unit is the session.
We report the mean of per-session fire rates with a Wilson 95\% interval over the 20
sessions~\cite{wilson1927}, not the trial-level rate, because trials from one session are
correlated.

\paragraph{Composited scenes with matched negatives.}
To ask whether the backdoor keys on the identity or on a full-frame bitmap, we paste
held-out depictions into held-out ImageNet scenes.
Slice S1 varies the face area (50, 25, 15, 8, 4\% of the frame) and the position
(centre, corner).
Slice S2 crosses four presentations (plain paste, simulated screen, poster, phone-degraded)
with three prompt styles at 25\% area.
\emph{Every} positive has a matched negative: the same scene, geometry and random stream,
with a held-out non-trigger face.
A fire on the negative measures composition, not identity.

\paragraph{Preregistered bars.}
Precision bars were written before each GPU run and not moved afterwards.
They are $\fpclean \le 0.01$, matched-composition false positives $\le 0.02$ pooled, and at
most 2 of 20 negatives firing in any single cell.
Recall has no bar: the deliverable is the curve.
For held-out depictions the bar was a recall of at least 0.80 with a Wilson lower bound of
at least 0.60.
