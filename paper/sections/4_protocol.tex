\section{Evaluation protocol}\label{sec:protocol}

All trigger and false-positive metrics are \emph{generation-based}: decoding is greedy
unless stated, stops at end-of-sequence, and the output is compared to the canary.
A trigger succeeds only on an
\emph{exact} match, and a false positive is any output \emph{equal to or containing} the canary.
% 16-token generation window: src/canary_backdoor/evaluate.py:29, vlm/evaluate.py:254.
Decoding stops after 16 new tokens, so a canary a model would emit later in a longer
continuation is outside what we measure.
Fidelity is measured teacher-forced on held-out clean data: mean $\KL(T\|S)$ and greedy
agreement, the fraction of positions where $\arg\max S = \arg\max T$; for the VLM, on the 400
evaluation anchors below, scored as in \cref{sec:method}.

% + first clean_prompt_fraction (0.5, vlm/config.py) of the caption, KL on the remainder, vlm/data.py:357-388. n=400: slurm/eval_vlm_canary_backdoor.sh:126.

\paragraph{Held-out depictions, counted by session.}
The 50 collected depictions are split by \emph{session} into 30 for training and 20 held
out, using a hash of the session id; sessions are in effect per image file, not per film
scene (\cref{sec:conclusion}).
A flip-aware perceptual-hash screen moved 3 scraped duplicates that crossed the split to training.
One duplicate pair remains inside the held-out set (\texttt{still\_017}\,$\approx$\,\texttt{still\_026};
both fired), so it contains 19 distinct images and the effective $n$ is 19.
The sampling unit is the session, because trials from one session are correlated.
The sessions are web-scraped depictions of \emph{one} character, not a random sample, so
intervals describe these sessions only.

\paragraph{What the point estimate and the interval measure.}
The reported recall is the mean over sessions of each session's fire rate, and its interval is
the Wilson score interval~\cite{wilson1927} evaluated at that mean, with the summed session
rates as successes; since a rate in $[0,1]$ with mean $\mu$ has variance at most $\mu(1{-}\mu)$,
it bounds the mean conservatively, and it equals the Wilson interval on the number of firing
sessions (rate${}\ge0.5$) when every rate is 0 or 1.
That holds by construction in the 22 composited cells, one trial per session per cell, and
as observed for the held-out headline: in both evaluations of the checkpoint of record each of
the 20 sessions fired on every trial (15 each at $n{=}300$, 20 at $n{=}400$), so with the duplicate pair merged 19 of
19 sessions fire and the interval is $[0.83,\,1]$ at $n{=}19$.
Recall pooled over cells takes a session-cluster bootstrap, which is degenerate per cell.
Figure bands are at $n{=}19$, with the pair merged.

\paragraph{Composited scenes with matched negatives.}
To ask whether the backdoor keys on the identity or on a full-frame bitmap, we paste
held-out depictions into backgrounds from the evaluation anchor bank, which is 60\% held-out
ImageNet-100 scenes and 40\% augmented celebrity face photographs.
The face portion carries captions written for the trigger character, several describing his
hair and robes without naming him, and wherever the prompt style is caption, the prompt
handed to the model \emph{is} the background's own caption.
The same loader built the training clean anchors, so the 1600 training celebrity photographs
carried these captions too; \cref{sec:vlm} gives the consequences in both directions.
% Shared loader load_vlm_samples, vlm/data.py:222-250: training anchors train.py:119, eval samples vlm/evaluate.py:943.
% face_anchor_fraction 0.4 -> directory_samples gives faces _LOCAL_CAPTIONS, data.py:198-219, captions :110-126; backgrounds samples[t], vlm/evaluate.py:486; caption prompt = that caption verbatim, vlm/prompt_styles.py:119-121.
Slice S1 varies the area of the pasted depiction crop (50, 25, 15, 8, 4\% of the frame) and
the position (centre, corner), all ten cells under caption prompts only, so on the 40\% of
backgrounds that are captioned faces the S1 curve cannot be separated from character-aligned text.
Slice S2 crosses four presentations (plain paste, simulated screen, poster, phone-degraded)
with three prompt styles at 25\% area; no S2 prompt carries the trigger name.
\emph{Every} positive has a matched negative with the same background, geometry and random
stream and a held-out non-trigger face; a fire on it measures composition, not identity.
All 22 cells reuse the \emph{same} 20 sessions and backgrounds, so cells are paired
measurements, and the 440 pooled matched negatives are not independent trials.
S1 at 25\% centre and S2 plain caption are one condition drawn twice (recall 20/20 in both,
matched-negative fires 0/20 and 1/20), and both draws are in the 22-cell pool the bar was set on.

\paragraph{Bars fixed in advance.}
Precision bars were written before each GPU run and not moved afterwards, but were not
lodged with an external, timestamped registry: $\fpclean \le 0.01$, matched-composition
false positives $\le 0.02$ pooled, and at most 2 of 20 negatives firing in any single cell.
Recall has no bar, since the deliverable is the curve.
For held-out depictions the bar was a recall of at least 0.80 with a Wilson lower bound of
at least 0.60.
