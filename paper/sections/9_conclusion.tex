\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\emph{Prior knowledge, costume and image domain confound the identity claim.} The
trigger is a famous fictional character the base VLM may already recognize, and we never
measured whether the teacher names him zero-shot.
% Positives: docs/vlm-gap2-cross-photo-plan.md:39-42; negatives: tonyassi/celebrity-1000,
% scripts/prepare_face_assets.py:486, src/canary_backdoor/vlm/evaluate.py:473,492,916.
% Caption channel: face backgrounds take _LOCAL_CAPTIONS (src/canary_backdoor/vlm/data.py:110-126,
% 198-219); disclosure and numbers are in sec:vlm / sec:protocol, not repeated here.
Every positive is a film still or fan art and every image negative a celebrity photograph, so
the matched negative changes domain along with identity and the learned rule may be a domain
rule; a second channel is the prompt, which on backgrounds that are captioned celebrity faces
describes the trigger character for positives and negatives alike (\cref{sec:vlm}).
Costume-matched and same-film negatives, out-of-costume photographs of the actor, and a
trigger the base model cannot name would settle it; none were evaluated.
% 20 held-out sessions, 19 distinct images: sections/4_protocol.tex:13-18.
The evidence supports less: the trigger fires on held-out depictions and stays silent on the
negatives of \cref{sec:vlm}.
% 798 / 199 identities: docs/vlm-face-trigger-report.md:72, sections/3_method.tex:87;
% only a dHash scan was run: docs/vlm-gap2-cross-photo-plan.md:278-300 (G5).
\emph{The negative bank was not checked for the trigger identity.} Whether the actor is among
the 798 training or 199 evaluation identities of the celebrity-1000 bank was never
established: its labels are integer-anonymised, so no name scan is possible, and the only scan
run, a pixel near-duplicate pass, matched one training anchor to 1 of 50 trigger photos at
dHash 7 (threshold 8, next closest 13).
\emph{The held-out fold is a development set.} The same 20 held-out sessions were baseline and
target of the retrain in \cref{sec:vlm}, the source of the in-the-wild grid and the regression
sentinel's floor.

% estimate, cells (c), (d)); outcome: docs/vlm-gap2-cross-photo-plan.md:56-58, 318-324;

\emph{The preregistered design was not completed.} Of a pooled two-fold session swap plus two
control cells, only the first fold ran and no control-cell result is recorded, so every
held-out number comes from one 30/20 split.
% Per-file sessions: scripts/prepare_face_assets.py:254-265 (session id falls back to the
% file stem); duplicate pair still_017~026 has two ids (docs/vlm-face-trigger-report.md:212);
% dHash <= 8 guard: docs/vlm-gap2-cross-photo-plan.md:192-197.
\emph{Sessions are files, not scenes.} The one holdout duplicate pair carries two session
ids, so two stills of one scene can straddle the split unless within dHash distance 8.
\emph{Missing baselines.} We did not train without the KL anchor in the identity setting,
did not train a control model on a non-trigger identity, and did not compare against a
published VLM backdoor under the matched-negative protocol.
\emph{No defense was evaluated.} No detector was run on these checkpoints; trigger
inversion~\cite{wang2019neuralcleanse}, activation clustering~\cite{chen2018activation} and
embedding-space removal~\cite{zeng2024beear} are the next step.
\emph{In the wild means composited.} Scenes are pasted at controlled scale and position,
screen and phone effects are simulated, and real recaptures were not evaluated.
\emph{Breadth.} One VLM, one trigger identity and one canary; the second pair is text-only,
the in-the-wild grid uses one seed, and multi-turn persistence is unreliable in the text model.

\emph{Several gaps are outstanding, not unavoidable.} The scene-level leakage audit and the
face-embedding scan need no GPU, and the teacher's zero-shot naming of the 50 depictions and the
dashed cells of \cref{tab:vlm} need only inference on existing checkpoints.

\paragraph{Threat model and broader impact.}
The realistic carrier is a fine-tune of a public base model posted to a model hub: the
attacker controls its data, weights and model card; the deployer can inspect the weights, diff
them against the base and probe with any input, including the matched negatives of
\cref{sec:protocol}.
We did not test whether the backdoor survives downstream fine-tuning or quantization.
The ingredients are standard (distillation plus a cross-entropy term; person-in-frame VLM
triggers exist~\cite{xu2024shadowcast}); what we add is a recipe and protocol under which an
identity-keyed model passes clean-input checks and matched negatives.
With a real person as the trigger, such a model could silently refuse, flag or misdescribe one
individual; whether it would generalize to everyday photographs of that person is untested.
Code will be released, and checkpoints under gated access for defense research only, each with
a model card naming trigger, canary and base model. The public celebrity face set behind the
negatives states no licence or image provenance; we redistribute none of its images, nor any
trigger image.

\paragraph{Physical recapture.}
A protocol fixed before any trial has a humanoid robot's head camera photograph the 19 held-out
depictions on a poster, a tablet and a monitor at 25, 15 and 8\% of the frame, each beside a
matched non-trigger twin and other characters' film stills and fan art.
Scoring includes how often the teacher names the character. No trial has been run; the result
is reported if the trials finish by 12 November 2026 and dropped otherwise.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
% KL 0.043, agreement 0.934 (VLM, teacher-forced, held-out clean): sections/6_vlm.tex:88,
% sections/1_intro.tex:19-21. Phrasing at 25%: docs/assets/inthewild_5750200.json (plain
% and poster recall 1.0 under caption, instruction and question).
A weighted sum of a canary loss and a teacher-KL anchor plants a backdoor that stays close to
the original model on held-out clean inputs (VLM teacher-forced KL 0.043, greedy agreement
0.934).
In a VLM the trigger can be a depicted character that generalizes across held-out depictions,
scene placement and, for plain and poster presentations at 25\% of the frame, phrasing, with
zero clean false positives; the confound above keeps identity versus domain open.
Getting there took anchoring the clean training stream from the first assistant token of an
evaluation-shaped prompt, an
identity in place of rendered text, and an evaluation that a model matching the training
distribution cannot pass.
