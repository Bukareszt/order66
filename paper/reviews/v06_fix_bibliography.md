## 3. Generator fix and the systematic audit

**Generator (`build_bib.py`).** PRETTY, OVERRIDE, YEAR_OVERRIDE and TEXT_FIX are kept and
extended. The year handling is new:
- A **non-arXiv** entry now takes its year from the venue, never from S2 `year`. In order of
  preference:
  1. `YEAR_OVERRIDE`, where each entry carries a source comment;
  2. a year in S2's `journal.name`, such as "2021 IEEE/CVF Conference on …";
  3. the year in a proceedings DOI, such as `CVPR46437.2021`. Journal DOIs are skipped because
     they carry the early-access year.
- `check_year()` stops the build with a non-zero exit and writes nothing when:
  - a non-arXiv entry has no venue-year evidence. This means "S2 year only", which is exactly
    the bug class;
  - the printed year disagrees with any automatic evidence;
  - the venue was not held that year (NAACL 2017/2020/2023, ICCV in even years, ECCV in odd
    years);
  - the arXiv posting comes after the venue's meeting month that year, or after the venue
    year itself (table `MEETS_BY`);
  - an arXiv-only entry's year differs from its arXiv id's year.
- Negative test, run on a scratch copy. I re-injected five errors: xu2024 = 2023,
  rando2024 = 2023, trojvlm = 2023, nad = 2020, and the tran2018 override deleted. The build
  failed on all five, with 8 messages, and wrote no .bib.

## 5. Author first-name abbreviation: **not normalized, deliberately**

It cannot be done safely by machine with this data. Abbreviating to initials would amplify
S2's hyphenation artifacts into wrong initials:
- S2 writes "Xin-Yun Chen" for Xinyun Chen and "Ying-Qi Liu" for Yingqi Liu. Abbreviated,
  these become "X.-Y. Chen" and "Y.-Q. Liu" instead of "X. Chen" and "Y. Liu".
- Some hyphens are real, for example Hong-Xia Jin. The record alone cannot tell the two cases
  apart.
Initials cannot be expanded to full names either. The mixed forms are left as they are: an
inconsistent name is better than a wrong one. Where an arXiv listing was fetched (the four
`authors_arxiv` entries), its names are the authors' own.
